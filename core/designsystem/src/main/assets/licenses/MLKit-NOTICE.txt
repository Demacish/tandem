Google ML Kit
=============

Tandem uses two Google ML Kit libraries with their models bundled in the app, so they work
without Google Play services and entirely on the phone:

  com.google.mlkit:barcode-scanning 17.3.0   (barcodes on packages)
  com.google.mlkit:text-recognition 16.0.1   (Latin text on receipts)

ML Kit is provided by Google under the ML Kit Terms of Service:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Barcodes and receipts are read on the device; Tandem does not send those photos anywhere.

ML Kit contains open-source components. Google publishes their names and full licence texts
with each library (third_party_licenses.txt in the published packages on maven.google.com).
